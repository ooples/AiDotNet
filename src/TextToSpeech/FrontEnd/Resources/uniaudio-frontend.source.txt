uniaudio-uppercase.tsv.gz records Python Unicode 14.0.0 full uppercase mappings,
including expansions such as sharp-s and ligatures, as used by str.upper().
Uncompressed SHA-256: e3822c10113225ca4ff8515505375060502fce55c10153b56106a1aa60408a10

The UniAudio repository identifies its code as MIT-licensed in readme.md.
Its Kaldi English pronunciation lexicon derives from CMUdict, whose attribution
and redistribution terms are reproduced in uniaudio-cmudict-license.txt.

Resources preserve the original bytes; gzip timestamps are zero for reproducibility.
Rebuild with tools/reference-data/uniaudio_frontend_resources.py.
